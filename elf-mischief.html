Parent cards or rescue cards?

Parent cards explain the kit’s 12 next-morning setups. Rescue cards are the little play signs that come with the grabber. They’re themed extras, not additional planned scenes.

Which crafts are secret?

The bracelet and mask are for your child to make and wear. Only the potpourri craft is a secret gift for a parent. Those child activities are separate from the accessory-only kit.
