Give your elf a little gift each night

Your child opens a numbered surprise and leaves it in the elf gift box before bed. The next morning, they can find their elf wearing, using, or playing with their gift. Your child opens mini elf cookie cutters and tucks them into the gift box. By morning, the elf is making pretend cookies with play dough or modeling clay. For this example, supply your own play dough or modeling clay. Add an extra portion and a kid-size cookie cutter if you’d like your child to bake alongside their elf.
