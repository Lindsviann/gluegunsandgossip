A few little details

Is an elf doll included?

No. These concepts are accessories and activities for an elf you already own. The finished listing will specify exactly what comes in each packet.

How does the advent gift box work?

The child opens the matching numbered compartment or packet, reads the card, and leaves the miniature gift in the elf’s present box. A grown-up arranges the prop overnight. The final packaging format will be shown before orders open.

Are these part of the fundraiser?

No. These are Glue Guns & Gossip retail products. Gobble Grams remains a separate Who Crew fundraiser.

Can I order or pay now?

Not yet. We are checking the finished sample, exact contents, pricing, stock, and pickup plan before enabling orders. Please do not send payment for this preview.

Miniature accessories contain small parts. Adult supervision and setup are required. Keep away from children under 3 and pets. Final product-specific suitability and care information will accompany the finished kit.
